# Support

This document describes how customers get help with Acme Cloud.

## Support Channels

- **Community support** (Starter tier) — public forum, best-effort response,
  no guaranteed response time.
- **Email support** (Growth tier) — 1-business-day response target for
  general questions.
- **Dedicated escalation path** (Enterprise tier) — 15-minute response
  target for P1 issues, via a named account team; see
  `enterprise-plans.md`.

## Support Hours

Community and email support are staffed during business hours across three
regions (Americas, EMEA, APAC), providing effective 24/5 coverage. Enterprise
P1 escalation is staffed 24/7, tied to the on-call rotation described in
`on-call.md`.

## What Support Can Help With

Support can help with account access issues, billing questions (routed to
the billing team per `billing.md` for disputes), general product questions,
and reporting suspected bugs. Support engineers do not have direct
production database access; they escalate to on-call engineering for issues
that require it.

## Escalating to Engineering

If a support ticket appears to be a P1 or P2 production issue rather than a
how-to question, support escalates it directly into the incident process
described in `incident-response.md`, bypassing the normal ticket queue.

## Feature Requests

Feature requests submitted through support are logged in the product
backlog but are not guaranteed a response time or commitment to build.

## Data Deletion Requests

Requests to delete account data (see `data-classification.md`) are handled
through the standard support channel and confirmed within 5 business days.
