# Hiring

This document describes the hiring process for engineering roles at Acme
Cloud (other departments follow a similar, but not identical, process
managed by their own recruiting partners).

## Process Stages

1. Recruiter screen (30 minutes).
2. Hiring manager interview (45 minutes).
3. Technical interview loop: typically two technical sessions (coding and
   system design) plus one collaboration/behavioral session.
4. Debrief and decision, typically within 3 business days of the final
   interview.

## Interviewer Training

All interviewers must complete interviewer training and shadow at least two
interviews before conducting one independently, to reduce bias and improve
consistency across the hiring loop.

## Take-Home Assignments

Acme Cloud does not use take-home coding assignments for engineering roles,
favoring live collaborative exercises instead, since take-homes were found
to disadvantage candidates with less free time outside work.

## Referrals

Employees who refer a successful hire receive a referral bonus, paid out
after the new hire completes 90 days, mirroring the probationary period
implied by the review exclusion window in `performance-reviews.md`.

## Internal Transfers

Internal candidates can apply to open roles the same way external
candidates do, but typically skip the recruiter screen since a hiring
manager reference is already available internally.

## Offer Approval

All offers require sign-off from the hiring manager's director and from HR
before being extended, to ensure compensation banding consistency.
