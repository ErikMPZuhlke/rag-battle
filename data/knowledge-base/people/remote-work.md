# Remote Work

Acme Cloud is a hybrid-first company with a flexible remote work policy.

## Standard Policy

Employees may work remotely up to **4 days per week**; most teams designate
one anchor day per week for in-person collaboration, set by the team lead.
Fully remote roles (no anchor day requirement) are possible for some
positions and are noted explicitly in the job posting.

## Working From Another Country

Working remotely from a different country than your employment contract for
more than 20 consecutive days requires approval from HR and Legal, due to
tax and employment law implications. Short trips (under 20 days) do not
require approval but should be noted in the team calendar.

## Home Office Equipment

Acme Cloud provides a home office stipend to support remote work; see
`expenses.md` for the stipend amount and reimbursement process, and
`equipment.md` for company-provided hardware like laptops, which is separate
from the stipend.

## Core Hours

Regardless of location, all employees are expected to be reachable during
core hours (10:00–15:00 in their local time zone) for meetings and
collaboration, outside of pre-approved schedule exceptions.

## Remote On-Call

Remote work does not exempt engineers from on-call responsibilities. See
`on-call.md` for expectations around connectivity and response time while
on-call, which apply regardless of where you are working from.

## Office Access

Employees may use any Acme Cloud office regardless of which office they are
formally assigned to, using their badge, which is provisioned during
onboarding.
