# Onboarding

This document describes the first two weeks for a new Acme Cloud employee.

## Day One

New hires receive a company laptop and accounts for core systems (email,
chat, ticketing, source control, CI) automatically, provisioned the night
before their start date. This baseline access does not include anything
sensitive — see `access-control.md` for what counts as elevated access.

## First Week

- Meet with your manager to review your 30/60/90-day plan.
- Complete mandatory security awareness training (see `authentication.md`
  and `data-classification.md` for the topics covered).
- Shadow your onboarding buddy on at least one on-call handoff or code
  review to see real workflows in action.

## Elevated Access

Access beyond the day-one baseline — including production secrets
(`secrets-readers` group), the `platform-team` group, or the
`security-team` group — is not granted automatically during onboarding. New
engineers must request it explicitly through the process in
`access-control.md`, typically once their manager confirms it's needed for
their role.

## Equipment

Standard equipment (laptop, monitor, peripherals) is described in
`equipment.md`. Additional equipment requests go through the same process
described there.

## Remote Onboarding

Remote new hires receive equipment shipped in advance of their start date
and have their first week scheduled with extra one-on-one time to compensate
for the lack of in-office context; see `remote-work.md` for the ongoing
remote work policy.

## 90-Day Check-In

At 90 days, new hires have a formal check-in with their manager and HR to
discuss ramp-up progress, separate from the standard performance review
cycle described in `performance-reviews.md`.
