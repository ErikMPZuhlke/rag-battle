# Time Off

Acme Cloud offers a flexible time-off policy for full-time employees.

## Vacation

Employees accrue vacation based on tenure, starting at 20 days per year in
year one and increasing to 25 days after three years. Vacation must be
approved by a manager in advance, ideally at least two weeks ahead for
requests longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Sick Leave

Sick leave is separate from vacation and uncapped for short-term illness;
employees should notify their manager as soon as practical. Extended medical
leave follows local statutory requirements and is coordinated with HR.

## Public Holidays

Employees observe the public holidays of the country in which they are
employed, published each January in the HR portal.

## Parental Leave

Acme Cloud offers 16 weeks of paid parental leave for all parents,
regardless of gender or which parent is the primary caregiver, in addition
to any statutory leave required locally.

## Time Off During On-Call

Employees cannot be scheduled for primary on-call during pre-approved
vacation; see `on-call.md` for how the rotation schedule accounts for time
off.

## Unlimited PTO

Acme Cloud does not offer unlimited PTO; time off is tracked and accrued as
described above, since the accrual model was found to produce more
consistent time-off usage across the company than unlimited policies.
