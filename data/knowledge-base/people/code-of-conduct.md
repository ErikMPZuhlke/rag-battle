# Code of Conduct

Acme Cloud expects all employees, contractors, and interns to uphold a
respectful, professional, and inclusive workplace.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect regardless of
  role, background, or seniority.
- Give and receive feedback constructively and assume good intent.
- Protect confidential company and customer information, per
  `data-classification.md`.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, retaliation against someone who raises a
concern in good faith, and falsification of company records are all
grounds for disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

## Reporting Concerns

Concerns can be raised to a manager, HR, or through an anonymous third-party
ethics hotline. Reports are investigated promptly and confidentially, and
retaliation against a good-faith reporter is itself a violation of this
code.

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose potential conflicts of interest (e.g., a close
relationship with a vendor, or outside employment with a competitor) to
their manager and HR as soon as they arise.

## Gifts and Hospitality

Employees may accept modest gifts (under $100 in value) from vendors or
partners but must decline anything larger and report any that are offered.
This applies especially to employees involved in `vendor-management.md`
decisions.

## Enforcement

Violations are reviewed case by case by HR, with input from the employee's
manager and, for serious matters, Legal. Consequences range from a coaching
conversation to termination, depending on severity and history.
