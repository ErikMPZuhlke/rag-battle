# Performance Reviews

Acme Cloud runs a structured performance review cycle twice a year.

## Cycle

Reviews happen every six months (typically March and September). Each cycle
includes self-assessment, peer feedback (2–4 peers chosen with manager
input), and a manager assessment.

## Ratings

Employees receive one of four overall ratings: Exceeds Expectations, Meets
Expectations, Needs Improvement, or Unsatisfactory. Ratings inform
compensation decisions but are not the sole input — see the compensation
review process, which is documented separately from this handbook.

## Calibration

Manager ratings are calibrated across each department before being finalized,
to ensure consistency in how ratings are applied across different teams and
managers.

## New Hires

Employees who joined less than 90 days before a review cycle are excluded
from that cycle and reviewed in the following one instead; see
`onboarding.md` for the separate 90-day check-in that happens regardless.

## Promotions

Promotions can be requested by a manager at any point in the cycle but are
formally approved only during the calibration process, twice a year, to keep
leveling consistent across the company.

## Performance Improvement Plans

Employees rated Needs Improvement or Unsatisfactory in two consecutive
cycles are typically placed on a formal Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
with specific, time-boxed goals defined jointly by the manager and HR.
