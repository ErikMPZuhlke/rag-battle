# Equipment

This document covers company-provided equipment, separate from the home
office stipend described in `expenses.md`.

## Standard Issue

Every employee receives a company laptop (choice of two standard models) and
a monitor on their start date, provisioned as part of onboarding (see
`onboarding.md`). Laptops are refreshed every 3 years or sooner if hardware
fails.

## Additional Equipment

Additional peripherals (keyboard, mouse, headset, second monitor) can be
requested through the IT portal and are typically approved automatically up
to a standard equipment budget; requests above that budget need manager
approval.

## Equipment for Remote Employees

Remote employees receive the same standard equipment, shipped to their home
address before their start date. Equipment for remote employees is company
property in the same way as for in-office employees, and must be returned or
accounted for at offboarding.

## Loss or Damage

Lost or damaged equipment is replaced by IT; employees are not personally
billed for accidental damage, but repeated losses may prompt a conversation
with the employee's manager.

## Personal Devices

Personal devices are not permitted to access production systems or
Confidential/Restricted data (see `data-classification.md`), even with MFA,
because they are not enrolled in the company's device management system.

## Offboarding

All company equipment must be returned within 10 business days of an
employee's last day, coordinated with the IT offboarding checklist described
in `access-control.md`.
