# Expenses

This document describes how employees are reimbursed for work-related
expenses.

## Home Office Stipend

Employees working remotely (see `remote-work.md`) receive a **$500/year**
home office stipend for furniture, monitors, or other office setup items not
provided directly by the company. The stipend requires manager approval
before purchase and is reimbursed via the expense portal within **30 days**
of submission. It renews each calendar year and does not roll over if
unused.

This stipend is separate from company-issued equipment like laptops, which
are provided directly rather than reimbursed — see `equipment.md`.

## Travel Expenses

Business travel (customer visits, conferences, team offsites) is booked
through the corporate travel tool where possible. Reasonable meal expenses
while traveling are reimbursed up to a daily cap that varies by city
cost-of-living tier.

## Conference and Training Budget

Each employee has an annual $1,500 learning and development budget covering
conferences, courses, and books, approved by their manager. Unused budget
does not carry over to the next year.

## Submission Process

All expenses must be submitted with a receipt within 60 days of the expense
being incurred. Expenses submitted after 60 days require an exception
approved by a manager and finance.

## Non-Reimbursable Items

Alcohol (outside of a client-facing meal), personal entertainment, and
expenses without a valid receipt are not reimbursable, except where local
law requires otherwise.
