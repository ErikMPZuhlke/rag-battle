# Refunds

> **Note:** The refund window described below was updated. See
> `deprecation-policy.md` for the current policy — this document retains
> historical context for support agents handling older cases.

## Previous Policy (Deprecated June 2025)

Historically, customers had **14 days** from the billing date to request a
refund for a subscription charge, provided the account had not exceeded a
light usage threshold during that period. This 14-day policy was deprecated
in favor of a longer window — see `deprecation-policy.md` for the current
30-day policy and its effective date.

## What Refunds Cover

Refunds apply to subscription charges (the recurring plan fee). They do not
cover usage-based overage charges, which are billed for consumption that
already occurred and are considered non-refundable except in cases of a
confirmed billing error.

## Billing Errors

If Acme Cloud made a billing error (e.g., double-charged a customer, or
charged after a cancellation was already processed), the incorrect charge is
refunded in full regardless of how much time has passed, once the error is
confirmed by the billing team.

## How to Request a Refund

Customers request refunds through the standard support channel described in
`support.md`. Refund requests are typically processed within 5 business
days and returned to the original payment method.

## Enterprise Refunds

Enterprise customers with a negotiated contract follow the refund terms in
their order form, which take precedence over the self-serve policy described
here; see `enterprise-plans.md`.
