# Enterprise Plans

Enterprise is Acme Cloud's top subscription tier, designed for large
customers with custom requirements.

## What's Included

- Unlimited team members and custom data retention up to 2 years (see
  `pricing.md`).
- A dedicated account team, including a named customer success manager.
- The SLA commitments described in `sla.md`, including the 99.9% uptime
  commitment for P1-affecting outages.
- SAML 2.0 single sign-on (see `authentication.md`).
- Optional EU-only data residency for customers with regulatory requirements
  (see `data-classification.md`).

## Contracting

Enterprise plans are sold via a negotiated order form and master service
agreement rather than the standard self-serve terms. Pricing, contract
length, and refund terms are set per customer in the order form, which takes
precedence over the default policies described in `refunds.md` and
`subscriptions.md`.

## Security Reviews

Enterprise prospects may request a copy of Acme Cloud's most recent
penetration test summary and SOC 2 report as part of their procurement
security review, per `vulnerability-management.md`.

## Onboarding

Enterprise customers get a guided onboarding process with a dedicated
solutions engineer, typically completed within the first 30 days of the
contract.

## Support Escalation

Enterprise customers have access to a dedicated escalation path with a
15-minute response target for P1 issues, tighter than the standard support
tiers described in `support.md`.
