# Trial Accounts

Acme Cloud offers a free trial so prospective customers can evaluate the
product before subscribing.

## Trial Terms

The trial runs for **14 days** and includes full Growth-tier functionality,
without requiring a credit card at signup. Trial accounts are limited to a
smaller data ingestion volume than a paid Growth account, to control cost
while still allowing meaningful evaluation.

## Trial Extensions

Sales can extend a trial by up to 14 additional days for prospective
Enterprise customers who need more time to evaluate integrations. Extensions
are not available self-service and must be requested through the sales
team.

## Conversion

At the end of the trial, the customer is prompted to add a payment method
and select a plan (see `pricing.md`). If no plan is selected, the account is
automatically suspended (not deleted) for 30 days, after which trial data is
deleted per `data-classification.md`.

## Trial Data

Trial account data is treated with the same security and privacy controls as
paid account data — there is no reduced protection for trial data.

## Abuse Prevention

Acme Cloud limits the number of trial accounts a single email domain can
create in a 90-day period to prevent abuse of the trial's ingestion
allotment. Legitimate customers needing multiple trials (e.g., for separate
business units) can request an exception from sales.
